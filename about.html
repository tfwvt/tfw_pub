About Triggerfish

Small studio. Long memory. Serious craft.

Triggerfish started in 1999 as Triggerfish Design, creating high-impact websites, graphics and multimedia when the web was still finding its feet. Since then we’ve shipped everything from Flash-era players and campaign sites to museum networks and live data applications.

The tools have changed many times. What hasn’t: we sweat the details, we tell clients the truth, and we build things that keep working long after launch.

25+Years designing and building for the web
19Projects in our current portfolio, from websites to touring exhibits
4Disciplines in one team: strategy, design, code and hardware
What we believe

Interactive means it has to work for real people.

A kid at a museum ATM, a caregiver logging a visit on a tablet, a political junkie refreshing a Senate map on election night — our work is judged by people who don’t care how it was built. So we test with them, design for them and keep things simple underneath.

  • Clarity over cleverness. The best interface is the one nobody has to think about.
  • Built to be maintained. Clean code, real documentation and a CMS your team can run.
  • Honest partnership. Straight answers on scope, cost and trade-offs from day one.
Our story

Two decades of shipping.

A few milestones from a long list of projects for clients who keep coming back.

  1. 1999

    Triggerfish Design opens

    Web sites, graphics and multimedia for clients increasing their mindshare online.

  2. 2008

    Statewide campaign

    A database-driven site for Scudder Parker’s run for Governor of Vermont.

  3. 2009

    Into the museum

    RFID interactives for Johnstown’s steel-mill exhibit, and a session archive for IFT’s annual meeting.

  4. 2010

    Energy Lab hits the road

    Touch tables and interactives for the National Guard’s touring exhibit.

  5. 2014

    Video goes online

    PhoebeTV launches its network of original web series.

  6. 2016

    Mapping elections

    Work on 270toWin’s interactive electoral maps begins.

  7. 2026

    Markets meet maps

    Live Kalshi prediction-market data arrives on 270toWin for the midterms.
